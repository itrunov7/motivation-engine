# /corpora — Harvested datasets

## What lives here

Datasets harvested from external sources (see `/sources/sources.json`):
interface screens and flows, science papers and effect sizes, reviews.
Each corpus will live in its own subfolder with a manifest describing its
source, harvest date, and license.

Empty at baseline — no scrapers or connectors exist yet, and building them
is explicitly out of scope for this stage. The app renders the corpus blocks
as `planned` while this folder is empty.

## Filled by

Future source connectors (per-source, gated by legal notes in sources.json).

## Phase

August–September (connectors phase).
